# Refund Policy

**Ican Apps** · Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy covers payments for full-access passes on **https://learn.icanapps.com** (Spelling Bee and Language Learning).

## 1. What you're paying for
A full-access pass is a **one-time payment** that unlocks all word lists in one app for your account. Because it's
digital content that's available as soon as you pay, please try the free word lists first to make sure the app suits you.

## 2. When we give a refund
We will refund your payment in full if, within **7 days** of paying:
- you were **charged more than once** for the same pass;
- you paid but the pass **was not unlocked**, and we can't fix it within 3 working days of you contacting us; or
- a technical fault on our side means the paid content **can't be used**, and we can't fix it.

Other refund requests made within 7 days are considered case by case. For example, you may have paid by mistake and not
yet used the paid lists.

## 3. When we don't give a refund
- After 7 days from the payment date.
- When the paid content has been used and works as described.
- When an account is suspended for breaking our [Terms of Service](terms.md).

## 4. How to ask for a refund
Email **icaninstitute.org@gmail.com** with:
- the email address of your account;
- the **payment reference** (it starts with `SB-` for Spelling Bee or `LL-` for Language Learning, and appears in
  your Paystack receipt); and
- the reason for your request.

We reply within 3 working days.

## 5. How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the **original payment method** through Paystack (card, bank or mobile money). Depending on
your bank or network, it can take **5–10 working days** to appear. When a pass is refunded, the paid lists are locked again.
