# Privacy Policy

**Ican Apps** · Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Ican Apps collects on **https://learn.icanapps.com** and how we use it.
We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

## 1. What we collect
- **Account details:** name, email address and password (stored encrypted), and the role you choose when you sign up.
- **Learning progress:** which word lists you've completed, quiz and spelling results, and stories read.
- **Payment records:** for each payment, the amount, currency, date, status and Paystack reference. **We do not
  receive or store card numbers, mobile money PINs or bank details.** Paystack handles those.
- **Technical data:** standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) and a login session cookie.
  Visitors who aren't signed in may have their progress saved only in their own browser (local storage).

## 2. How we use it
- To create and run your account, and to save your progress.
- To process payments and unlock the pass you bought.
- To show teachers the progress of the learners in their care.
- To answer your questions and keep the site secure.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with
- **Paystack**, to process payments. Their privacy policy applies to the details you enter on their checkout.
- **Our hosting provider**, which stores the site's data on our behalf.
- Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## 4. Children
The site is designed for children, used with a parent, guardian or teacher. We collect only what's needed to run an
account and track learning. A parent or guardian can ask us to see, correct or delete a child's data at any time.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep account and progress data while the account is active. Payment records are kept as long as accounting
and tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your account.

## 6. Security
Passwords and payment keys are stored encrypted, and the site uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but
we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## 7. Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address
below. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
